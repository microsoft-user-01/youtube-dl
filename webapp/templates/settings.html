How to Export Cookies

1Install Extension

Install a cookie export extension like Get cookies.txt LOCALLY (Chrome) or cookies.txt (Firefox)

2Login to YouTube

Go to youtube.com and make sure you're logged in to your account.

3Export Cookies

Click the extension icon while on YouTube, then click "Export" or "Get cookies.txt". Copy the entire contents.

4Paste & Save

Paste the cookies above and click "Save Cookies". The cookies will be used for all downloads.
